MDP Theme: The Odyssey

Premise: The Captain has two missions, but no ship nor a crew. He recruit specialists, and together they assemble the vessel and prepare for the voyage.

World:
- Robot → The boat
- Obstacles → Cyclops (wherever the Cyclops is facing is the orientation of the obstacle)
- Carpark → Ithaca
- Arena → Unknown Seas
- Computed route → The Voyage

Core roles (video introduction in this order)
1) RPi → Captain
- Starts the story by presenting the missions (Tasks 1 & 2)
- Connects the different systems together
- Handles vision and identifies targets

2) STM → Shipwright / Engineer
- Builds the "boat"
- Makes it capable of moving
- Handles the physical control side of the vessel

3) Android → Cartographer
- Creates the map
- Records Cyclopes and their gazes
- Records discoveries
- Tracks the vessel

4) Algorithm → Navigator
- Takes the Cartographer's map
- Determines the order/route
- Charts the path through the Cyclopes

Task 1 → The Trials
1. Navigate between the Cyclopes
2. Face their gaze
3. Identify each target
4. Record discoveries
5. Complete the voyage

Task 2 → The Homecoming
1. Depart Ithaca
2. Follow the directions encountered to reach destination (Troy?)
3. Return to Ithaca
